PRIVACY

Privacy at Brain Rush

What we collect

Brain Rush does not require an account for its core tools. Calculator, text, password, percentage, BMI, date, random number, case conversion and color tools are designed to process input locally in your browser.

Third-party services

The currency converter sends the requested currency pair and amount to the public Frankfurter exchange-rate service. The QR generator loads QRCode.js from cdnjs when the QR tool is opened.

Future analytics and advertising

If analytics, advertising or other third-party services are added, this page should be updated before those services are enabled. Any required consent and disclosure controls should be implemented at that time.

Contact

For privacy questions, add the project's official contact address here before publishing the site publicly.
